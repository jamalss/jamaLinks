Meekness

Not weakness — a strength that chooses to yield.

Scroll

Why a leaf

A maple leaf does not resist the season. It does not cling to the branch out of fear, and it does not fall because it has stopped mattering.

It turns brilliant, then lets go — patient, unresisting, trusting the roots beneath it. That is meekness: not the absence of strength, but strength held loosely.

What the word means +

Meekness is the quality of being patient in the reception of injuries; it stands opposed to malice and revenge.
